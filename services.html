Therapy Categories Explained

Not every guest knows how to choose between Ayurvedic therapy, modern spa-style massage, and skin-oriented treatments. These quick sections make that choice easier.

Traditional Ayurveda

For guests seeking Kerala wellness identity

Herbal Ayurveda, Sampoorna Ayurveda, Marma massage, Kalari massage, Shirodhara, and Kizhi therapies appeal to travellers who want a deeper Ayurvedic experience and a more traditional treatment feeling.

Relaxation & Spa

For comfort, calmness, and travel recovery

Relaxing massage, aroma massage, Swedish massage, hot stone massage, candle massage, and steam bath support are ideal for guests who mainly want to unwind and feel restored after travel.

Focused Care

For shorter or more specific body support

Head and shoulder massage, back and neck massage, sports massage, and deep tissue massage are useful for guests who have limited time or are dealing with more concentrated body fatigue.
